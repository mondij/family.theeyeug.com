International travel with kids involves a layer of paperwork and preparation that domestic trips don't — passports for every family member, potential consent letters, and health considerations that vary by destination. None of it is complicated on its own, but it does need more lead time than most families expect.

This guide focuses on the practical, easy-to-miss requirements rather than general packing or itinerary advice, which we cover elsewhere.
