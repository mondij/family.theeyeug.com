Flying with kids has an outsized reputation for disaster relative to how most flights actually go — but the flights that go well usually have a handful of small decisions behind them, made before boarding rather than improvised in the air.

This guide covers those decisions by age group, from booking through landing, plus a level-headed take on what to do when a flight doesn't go to plan.
