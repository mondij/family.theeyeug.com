A full family packing list is really five smaller lists — documents, health, clothing, entertainment, and tech — and most of the stress around packing comes from trying to hold all five in your head at once instead of working through them one at a time.

This is the complete version, meant to be worked through a week or so before a longer trip. If you're looking for the fast, night-before version instead, we've got a shorter companion guide for that.
