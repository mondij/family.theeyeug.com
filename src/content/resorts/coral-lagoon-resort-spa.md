Coral Lagoon Resort & Spa is an all-inclusive property built around a single idea: a calm, shallow lagoon pool that works for the youngest kids in the group without boring everyone else. It won't suit families chasing dramatic beaches or nightlife, but for a low-effort week with toddlers or preschoolers, it's one of the more thoughtfully designed resorts we've reviewed in the region.
