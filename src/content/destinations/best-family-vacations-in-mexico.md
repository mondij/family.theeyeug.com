For a lot of North American families, Mexico is the first international trip with kids — and the Riviera Maya in particular has built an entire industry around making that trip easy. All-inclusive resorts here do more of the planning for you than almost anywhere else in the world.

This guide covers the areas worth choosing between, what's actually worth doing beyond the resort pool, and the handful of practical details that catch first-time visitors out.
