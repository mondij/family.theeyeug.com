The idea that European city trips don't work with young kids usually comes from trying to do a European city trip the way you would without them — long museum days, dinner reservations at 9pm, and a walking pace built for adults. Done differently, several European cities are genuinely excellent family destinations.

This list sticks to cities that hold up well at a family's pace, with specific sights and neighborhoods that make the difference rather than a generic "here's what to see" rundown.
