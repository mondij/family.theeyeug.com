National parks don't get pitched as family destinations as often as beaches or theme parks, but the right ones deliver something those can't: genuine wildlife, real quiet, and a sense of scale that tends to stick with kids longer than a rollercoaster does.

This guide is deliberately selective — not every one of the 63 US national parks is a good match for a family trip, and we'd rather point you toward the handful that reliably work than list all of them and let you find out the hard way.
