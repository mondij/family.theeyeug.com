California is the destination families reach for when one theme park trip isn't quite enough, or when the plan is a proper road trip rather than a single home base. That flexibility is the appeal — and also the thing that trips people up when they try to fit too much of the state into too short a trip.

This guide breaks the state into the regions that actually make sense to base a family trip around, with an honest read on which is the best fit depending on your kids' ages and how much driving you're willing to do.
