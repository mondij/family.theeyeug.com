Japan doesn't usually make a first-time list of "easy" family destinations, but it deserves to — the trains run on time, the streets are clean and safe late into the evening, and there's a genuine abundance of things built to delight kids specifically, not just tolerate them.

This guide focuses on the cities and pacing that make a Japan trip work well with kids, rather than trying to fit an ambitious adult itinerary into a shorter, kid-adjusted version of the same trip.
