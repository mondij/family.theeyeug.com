Italy rarely tops "easiest family destination" lists, and it's true that a full adult-paced Italian itinerary — three cities, a museum a day, dinner at 9pm — doesn't translate well to traveling with kids. Slowed down and rebuilt around food, piazzas, and one or two bases rather than a circuit, it works considerably better than its reputation suggests.
