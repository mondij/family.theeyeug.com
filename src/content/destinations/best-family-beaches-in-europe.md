Europe's beach destinations get sold on scenery, and the scenery is real — but scenery and toddler-friendly are two different things. Some of the most photographed coastlines in Europe are also some of the least practical for a family with young kids.

This guide points toward the beach towns that get the balance right: still beautiful, still authentically European, but with the shallow water, sandy entry, and easygoing pace that make a beach trip actually relaxing with kids along.
