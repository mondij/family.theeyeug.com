"Caribbean" covers dozens of islands with very different personalities, and treating them as interchangeable is how families end up somewhere that doesn't fit — a beautiful but rocky beach when they needed shallow sand, or a quiet island when the kids needed more to do than swim.

This guide breaks the region down by what actually matters for a family trip: water conditions, resort infrastructure, and how much there is to do beyond the beach.
