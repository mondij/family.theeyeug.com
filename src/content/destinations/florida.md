Florida is, for a lot of families, the first real vacation — and for good reason. It's one of the few destinations where a toddler, a ten-year-old, and a teenager can all come home saying it was their favorite trip, without anyone having to compromise the whole time.

This guide covers where to base yourselves, what's actually worth doing with kids, and the handful of things that catch first-time visitors out.
